## Original narration
What happens when lava meets the ocean? Seawater flashes into steam as molten rock rapidly chills and shatters into glassy fragments. But the white cloud isn't harmless fog. It can contain hydrochloric acid and tiny shards of volcanic glass. Scientists call it laze. And the new land forming at the shoreline can collapse without warning, sometimes triggering violent explosions. Lava doesn't boil the whole ocean. It creates new coastline — with serious dangers hidden inside the steam. Scenario Files. Beyond the Known.

## Science / visual guardrails
USGS: lava-ocean interaction produces rapidly cooled volcanic glass fragments, steam, laze plume containing hydrochloric acid and microscopic glass.
