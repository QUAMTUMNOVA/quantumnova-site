## Original narration
What would rain look like on Titan, Saturn's largest moon? Titan has clouds, rivers, lakes and weather. But at nearly minus one hundred and eighty degrees Celsius, it's far too cold for liquid water on the surface. Instead, methane can evaporate, form clouds and fall as rain. Drops descend through a thick orange haze, darkening frozen dunes and feeding hydrocarbon rivers and lakes. There are even seas of liquid methane and ethane. It looks strangely like Earth, until you remember the rain is fuel, not water. Scenario Files. Beyond the Known.
