## Scientific foundation
Set **INSIDE a pressurised spacecraft**, not in vacuum: A carbonated drink still releases CO2, but absent normal buoyancy gas bubbles do not rise through liquid. Soda can foam or form floating blobs, and the undissolved gas/liquid mix can contribute to a "wet burp". Real spaceflight soda-container experiments occurred on the Space Shuttle in the 1980s.
