## Science
A sufficiently powerful lightning strike on silica-rich sandy ground can fuse sand grains along electrical path into a **fragile glass-lined, often hollow, irregular branching tube**, a *fulgurite*. Not guaranteed at all strikes, exterior often rough and encrusted. Sources:
- NOAA NSSL https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/education/svrwx101/lightning/faq/
- Oxford University Museum of Natural History https://www.oumnh.ox.ac.uk/fulgurites
- US National Park Service https://www.nps.gov/articles/grsa-fulgurites.htm
