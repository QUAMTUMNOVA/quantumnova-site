Narration draft (approx 30 seconds):

What happens when lightning strikes sand? The electric current can heat the ground enough to melt silica grains almost instantly. Along its path, the sand fuses, then cools into a fragile tube of natural glass. It's called a fulgurite — sometimes nicknamed petrified lightning. Not every strike makes one. And it isn't a polished crystal. Its sandy exterior can hide a glass-lined, hollow interior. A split-second bolt, preserved underground. Scenario Files. Beyond the Known.
