## Original narration
What happens if you jump on the Moon? You don't float away. Lunar gravity is about one-sixth Earth's. With the same takeoff speed, you could jump roughly six times higher and stay in the air much longer. But a bulky spacesuit makes jumping harder, and landing badly can still hurt. That's why Apollo astronauts often used an unusual hopping gait to move across the surface. Dust falls in graceful arcs, without air to make it billow. The Moon hasn't switched gravity off. Every movement simply feels different. Scenario Files. Beyond the Known.
