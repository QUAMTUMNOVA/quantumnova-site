# Scenario Files #007 — What Happens to an Air Bubble in the Deep Ocean?

**Audience:** general science curious; not a child-directed channel. Owner authorized two more Shorts to be completed and scheduled before next long EP005.

## Fraser narration
What happens to an air bubble deep in the ocean? Imagine taking one down inside a transparent research chamber. Every ten metres adds roughly another atmosphere of pressure. At ten metres, the pressure is already about double what it is at the surface. So a bubble containing the same gas would shrink to around half its volume. Go deeper and it gets smaller still; some gas may dissolve into the water. But a bubble rising naturally does the reverse. It expands as pressure falls. Tiny bubble, enormous physics. Scenario Files.
