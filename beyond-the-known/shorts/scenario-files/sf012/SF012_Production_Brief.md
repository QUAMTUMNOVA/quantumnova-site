## Narration
How can a blown-out candle light itself again? That rising white trail isn't just ordinary smoke. The hot wick is still releasing wax vapour and tiny droplets. The vapour is fuel. When a flame meets that fuel-rich plume, ignition can travel back down towards the wick, lighting the candle again. It's not a flame jumping through empty air. It's a brief, moving pathway of burning wax gas. And once the plume disperses, the trick no longer works. Scenario Files. Beyond the Known.
